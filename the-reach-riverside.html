My work with The Reach Riverside spans brand development, creative direction, digital design and marketing strategy, focusing on establishing a distinct visual identity and strengthening the restaurant's positioning within London's hospitality market.

I designed and built the restaurant's new website, created brand communications and digital campaigns, and developed creative concepts to support footfall, seasonal promotions and private events.

the project, in short

the challenge

Reposition The Reach as The Reach Riverside, with a distinct identity and a clear place in London's hospitality market.

the insight

Competitor and audience analysis showed a gap for Mediterranean and continental dining on the riverside at Fulham Reach.

the decision

Position the restaurant in that gap: the river is Mediterranean, and so are we. A new name, identity and website say it everywhere a guest meets the brand.

what I did

Wrote the brief, designed the identity and built the Wix site. Then designed brand communications and campaigns for footfall, seasonal promotions and private events.

the result

1k → 6k accounts engaged in the two weeks after launch.
