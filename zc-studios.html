ZC Studios is an AI-native creative practice I founded, bringing together visual design, cultural research, brand strategy and emerging technologies. Built using Claude Code and Codex, the studio explores how AI can support and expand independent creative production without replacing human creative direction.

Since May, I've developed a system of 13 AI agents and an automated workflow designed to streamline research, development and production. By delegating repetitive and technical processes, I've created more space for creative thinking, experimentation and developing my design practice, while learning to orchestrate AI tools as part of an integrated creative workflow.
