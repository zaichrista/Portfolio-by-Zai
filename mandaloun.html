A redesign of the Mandaloun Westfield website, starting from the customer journey: how someone goes from discovering the restaurant to booking a table.

I reworked the information architecture, navigation and menu presentation around that route, and I'm building more flexible content structures so the menus are easy to keep up to date.

the project, in short

the challenge

The site had no clear call to action, so visitors had no obvious route to booking a table.

the insight

Mapping the journey from discovery to booking showed where it broke: there was no clear call to action.

the decision

Lead with the menu, and make booking the obvious next step from it.

what I did

Mapped the customer journey to booking, briefed a menu-led redesign, and reworked information architecture, navigation and menu presentation.

the result

A redesign in progress. The preview shows the new version, and it hasn't been measured yet.
